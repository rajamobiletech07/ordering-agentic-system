# Purchase Ordering Policy

## 1. Scope
This policy governs replenishment purchase orders for stores and distribution centers.

## 2. Order creation
- Every order must reference an approved vendor and an active SKU.
- Quantities should be rounded to the vendor case pack.

## 3. Approval thresholds
- Orders up to **USD 25,000** may be confirmed by the requesting buyer.
- Orders above USD 25,000 require a manager signature.

## 4. Changes and cancellations
- Orders may be cancelled any time before delivery.
