# Vendor Management Rules

## Approved vendors
Only vendors with status *active* in the vendor master may receive purchase orders. New vendors require
onboarding by the Vendor Management Office, including tax documentation and a food-safety certificate for
perishable suppliers.

## Lead times
- The quoted vendor lead time is measured in calendar days from order submission to delivery at the
  receiving location.
- Perishable dairy and produce vendors must deliver within **3 days**; ambient grocery vendors within 7 days.
- If a vendor misses the quoted lead time on more than 10% of orders in a quarter, their rating is reduced
  and buyers should prefer an alternate vendor.

## Minimum order quantities and case packs
Vendors define a minimum order quantity (MOQ), usually one case pack. Orders below the MOQ are rejected by
the vendor portal; always round order quantities **up** to a whole number of case packs.

## Vendor ratings
Vendor ratings range from 0 to 5. Vendors rated below **3.0** are placed on a performance watch list and new
orders above USD 5,000 to them require approver confirmation.

## Payment terms
Standard payment terms are NET30. PREPAID vendors must be paid at order submission. Early-payment discounts
may be taken when documented in the vendor contract.
