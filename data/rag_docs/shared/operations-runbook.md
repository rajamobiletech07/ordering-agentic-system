# Ordering Operations Runbook

## Order cut-off times
Orders submitted before **14:00 local time** are transmitted to vendors the same day. Orders submitted after
the cut-off are transmitted the next business day, which adds one day to the expected delivery date.

## Receiving hours
Stores receive deliveries between 06:00 and 11:00. Distribution centers receive 24/7 with appointment
scheduling.

## Notifications
Order confirmations are emailed to the requesting buyer. Payment failures generate an email and an in-app
alert. Notification delivery is retried automatically up to five times.

## System outages
If the ordering system or a supporting service is unavailable, do not place duplicate orders by phone. Wait
for the service to recover and verify order status in the system of record before retrying.

## Escalation
Escalate stuck orders older than 24 hours to the Procurement Operations on-call rotation.
