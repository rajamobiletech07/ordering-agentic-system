# Acme Retail Ordering Addendum

## Approval threshold
For Acme Retail the approval threshold is **USD 10,000** per order. Orders above this value require an Acme
user with the approver role, who must not be the requester.

## Payment limit
The Acme per-order payment limit is **USD 50,000**.

## Preferred vendors
Hill Country Dairy (VND-003) is the preferred dairy vendor for Texas and Midwest stores. Harbor Coffee
Roasters (VND-013) supplies all coffee to distribution centers.

## Weekend ordering
Dairy orders for Saturday delivery must be submitted by **Thursday 14:00**.
